| P41 | **A Krylov.jl wrapper as an optional extension of ImplicitSolvers.** A weak dependency on Krylov.jl and an extension `ImplicitSolversKrylovExt`. The algorithm types (for example `KrylovJL(:gmres)`, `:minres`, `:cg`, with `memory`, `itmax` and the preconditioners `M` and `N` as `ldiv!` objects or functions) are defined in the package, so callers can name them without loading Krylov.jl; the extension supplies their `linsolve` methods, and without it a call fails with a message that says to load Krylov.jl. The wrapper keeps the package's contract: D2's tolerance passed as Krylov.jl's `atol` and `rtol`, a final true-residual check (a solve Krylov.jl calls solved but whose true residual misses D2 reports `:stalled` or continues), a `SolverReport` with `nfe` from Krylov.jl's statistics, and P38's tangent correction for dual numbers. A workspace is reused across calls once Q11's `init`/`solve!` exists, and until then held in the algorithm object or built per call. The in-house GMRES and MINRES stay the default; the extension becomes the default only if P40 or Q17 gives a measured reason. Tests: the oracle cases of `lib/ImplicitSolvers.jl/test/krylov_oracle.jl` run through `linsolve` with both backends, and also `Float32`, `ForwardDiff.Dual`, complex duals and JLArrays with `allowscalar(false)`. Each case where Krylov.jl fails is recorded as `@test_broken`, not worked around. `bench/krylov_allocations.jl` measures the wrapper's allocations against `gmres!` alone | B | proposed | — | the maintainer, 2026-11-24 ("a Krylov.jl wrapper, as an ext and optional, unless we have reason to move to it"); [[Shared Solver Layer]] "Why not Krylov.jl" | Krylov.jl's workspaces, preconditioners and GPU support become available without making it a dependency of every package; running both backends on the same cases is the measurement P40 needs |
