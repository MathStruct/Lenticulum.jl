implementation

HardConstraintFactor(dims, m; noise = 0) (hard_constraint.jl): the linear relation ∑i​Ai​xi​=c imposed exactly, with extended Gaussian messages. What LinearConstraintFactor(…; noise = 1e-8) approximates. Its free energy is the finite limit of the noisy relation’s as the noise vanishes.

Sources: Stein & Samuelson, A Category for unifying Gaussian Probability and Nondeterminism, CALCO 2023 (Gaussian relations: a linear relation composed with Gaussian noise). code: hard_constraint.jl; the belief algebra in lib/LenticulumCore.jl/src/extended_gaussian.jl. tests: test/hard_constraint.jl, test/hard_plates.jl (plates and mean-field nodes, P6).

Bibliography: Stein & Samuelson 2023

Theory (CT-ML wiki): Gaussian Relations

Theory

The factor is the relation R={x:∑i​Ai​xi​=c}, optionally blurred by a noise ε∼N(0,Q) on its residual. Its message to channel t, given the other channels’ messages μi​, is the likelihood of xt​:

μf→t​(xt​)=∫1[At​xt​=c−∑i=t​Ai​xi​+ε]∏i=t​μi​(xi​)N(ε;0,Q)dxdε.

Written with extended Gaussians it is two exact operations. The joint of the inputs (independent, so block-diagonal) is pushed forward to the residual r=c−∑i=t​Ai​xi​+ε, an extended Gaussian on Rm. The message is then the preimage of r under At​: the likelihood xt​↦pr​(At​xt​),

μf→t​=preimage(pushforward(⨂i=t​μi​,−[Ai​]i=t​,c,Q),At​).

Both are exact for flat, hard and Gaussian directions (extended_gaussian.md), so nothing about the inputs needs special cases. An uninformative input makes r flat along its Ai​‘s range, the τ→∞ limit of constraint.md §4.1. A clamped input makes r hard there. null(At​) is flat in the message, as is anything the residual cannot see. With Q≻0 the message is the one LinearConstraintFactor computes; with Q=0 and point inputs it is the hard relation itself.

Free energy (2026-11-11)

Without noise the factor is f(x)=δ(Gx−c), the limit of the Gaussian relation N(Gx;c,εI) as ε→0; G=[Ai​]i​ stacks the channels’ coefficients. Its Bethe term Ff​=Ebf​​[−logf]−H(bf​) diverges, energy and entropy alike, but the term of the noisy relation has a finite limit, and that limit is the term used.

First the inputs are reduced. A point xj​=vj​ moves into the constant, c↦c−Aj​vj​, as LinearConstraintFactor does. An extended-Gaussian input with hard directions Hj​ is written xj​=x0j​+Uj​sj​ along the rest (Uj​ an orthonormal basis of Hj⊥​), which moves Aj​x0j​ into the constant and leaves the block Aj​Uj​. This gives G~s=c~ on k coordinates. The factor belief in s is bε​∝N(G~s;c~,εI)∏j​N−1(sj​;ηj​,Λj​). If G~ has full row rank m, then as ε→0:

  • the residual covariance tends to εI, so E[2ε1​r⊤r]→2m​;
  • the term 21​logdet(2πεI)=2m​log2πε grows without bound;
  • in coordinates along the solution set R={G~s=c~} (orthonormal, z) and across it, the entropy splits as HR​(bf​)+2m​log(2πeε)−21​logdet(G~G~⊤). Across R the belief has covariance ε(G~V)−1(G~V)−⊤, with V an orthonormal basis of G~‘s row space and ∣detG~V∣=det(G~G~⊤)1/2.

The divergent parts cancel (2m​+2m​log2πε−2m​log2πeε=0):

Ff​=21​logdet(G~G~⊤)−HR​(bf​),HR​(bf​)=2k−m​(1+log2π)−21​logdet(N⊤ΛN),

with N an orthonormal basis of null(G~) and Λ the inputs’ block-diagonal precision. HR​ is the entropy relative to Lebesgue measure on R, and a point has HR​=0.

For the sum over the graph to be −logZ on a tree, the other terms must measure entropy the same way: relative to Lebesgue measure on each belief’s own support. A variable that the relation fixes in some directions has variable_entropy = support_entropy of its extended Gaussian (LenticulumCore, extended_gaussian.md, “Entropy”). A GaussianFactor or GaussianPrior that receives a hard message substitutes its hard directions out, as above (lib/Lenticulum.jl/src/gaussian.md, “Free energy with hard inputs”). A clamped variable keeps H(δ)=0, the case of every direction hard.

That is not yet enough where two or more hard messages meet at one variable along non-orthogonal subspaces: each factor’s 21​logdet(G~G~⊤) is taken on a cavity the other messages have already cut, and the sum misses −21​[(p−1)logdetG−∑i​logdetG−i​] (G the Gram matrix of the messages’ hard bases; −logsinθ for two scalar projections). The variable’s term adds it (I26; Mycelium free_energy.md, “Exact messages that meet at a variable”).

The term is infinite, and refused, in two cases:

  • G~ has rank below m: redundant equations, or equations on a direction an input already fixes exactly (δ(0), two hard constraints on one direction);
  • N⊤ΛN is singular: the factor belief is flat along R, an improper posterior.

With a positive definite noise the term is LinearConstraintFactor’s.

Free energy with a singular noise (P5, 2026-11-17)

A noise Q⪰0 of rank 0<r<m is written Q=Qs​DQs⊤​, with Qs​ (m×r) an orthonormal basis of its range, D its positive eigenvalues, and Qh​ (m×(m−r)) an orthonormal basis of its null space; an eigenvalue counts as zero below LenticulumCore’s _eig_tol (the tolerance the messages use; isposdef would accept a rank-deficient BB⊤, whose zero eigenvalues come out as 10−16). The relation is the limit of the noise Qε​=Q+εQh​Qh⊤​, and since [Qs​ Qh​] is orthogonal its density factors exactly:

N(G~s;c~,Qε​)=N(Gs​s;cs​,D)N(Gh​s;ch​,εI),Gs​=Qs⊤​G~, cs​=Qs⊤​c~, Gh​=Qh⊤​G~, ch​=Qh⊤​c~.

The soft factor does not depend on ε, so it joins the inputs: the precision Λ′=Λ+Gs⊤​D−1Gs​ and information η′=η+Gs⊤​D−1cs​. The exact factor is the noise-free case above with (Gh​,ch​) for (G~,c~) and Λ′ for Λ, and the soft factor adds its average energy under the factor belief on R={Gh​s=ch​}:

Ff​=21​logdet(Gh​Gh⊤​)−HR​(bf​)+21​Ebf​​[r⊤D−1r]+21​logdet(2πD),r=Gs​s−cs​,

with HR​ from N⊤Λ′N (N a basis of null(Gh​)). Under bf​, s=s0​+Nz with s0​ any solution of Gh​s0​=ch​ and z∼N((N⊤Λ′N)−1N⊤(η′−Λ′s0​), (N⊤Λ′N)−1); the mean of s does not depend on the choice of s0​. With r=0 soft equations this is the noise-free term, and with r=m it is LinearConstraintFactor’s. The rank condition is on Gh​ alone: the soft equations may outnumber the free coordinates.

Graded as fit =21​E[r⊤D−1r], complexity =21​logdetGh​Gh⊤​+21​logdet2πD, negentropy =−HR​.

Derivatives. Q is a constant of the factor (Float64), so its split is taken once, on values, and carries no tangent. Gh​=Qh⊤​G~ carries the inputs’ tangents, and its SVD takes P4’s constant-rank terms as before; s0​=Gh⊤​(Gh​Gh⊤​)−1ch​ and the soft part are plain arithmetic, so ForwardDiff and Zygote go through them. islearnable stays false, so the row’s “learnable” means the derivatives of the other factors’ parameters through this term (test/hard_gradient.jl).

Implementation

  • The parameters and polarities are LinearConstraintFactor’s: (A, c), any one channel Unobserved(), the others Observed() or Latent(). noise defaults to 0 and may be any positive semidefinite m × m matrix (soft along its range, hard elsewhere).
  • Inputs become extended Gaussians by LenticulumCore.extended: a GaussianBelief (also improper), a DiracBelief, an ExtendedGaussian, or nothing (TrivialBelief(), no message: flat). A mixture (of Gaussians or of extended Gaussians) is collapsed to its total moments (moment_match), as LinearConstraintFactor collapses one: exact for the collapsed input, an assumed-density approximation for the mixture (P27, 2026-11-17; refused before).
  • The message is always an ExtendedGaussian, also when it could be a GaussianBelief, so its type does not depend on the data. Mycelium’s combine, belief_distance and damp take it (lib/Mycelium.jl/src/gaussian.md, “Extended Gaussians”), so the factor runs under any schedule.
  • islearnable is false. local_free_energy is the term above (fit 0, complexity 21​logdetG~G~⊤, negentropy −HR​), from the cavity messages (uses_cavity). The reduction is _reduced_relation in constraint.jl, shared with LinearConstraintFactor.

Plates (P6)

A plate of tied hard relations (factor_messages, one call for all replicas) shares the work when the replicas have one noise and one set of dimensions, and each channel’s inputs share their covariance and flat directions across the replicas: points, no message, TrivialBelief(), Gaussians of one precision (also improper, flat along its null space), extended Gaussians of one Σ and D. That is the case of data under a shared prior. Every decomposition in factor_message (the hard and flat directions of the pushforward r, its pseudo-inverse precision Λr​, the SVD of C=H⊤At​ and the precision on its null space) then depends on the covariances only, so replica 1 computes them, and each replica’s message has replica 1’s Σ and D with the mean

μi​=Πo​[PH⊤+G(At⊤​Λr​−Λp​PH⊤)]Πr​(c−∑n=t​An​mn,i​),

where mn,i​ is replica i‘s input mean on channel n, Πr​ projects onto the complement of the pushforward’s flat directions, H is its hard directions, P=C+, N a basis of null(C), Λp​=At⊤​Λr​At​, G=N(N⊤Λp​N)+N⊤, and Πo​ projects onto the complement of the message’s flat directions. This is _from_potential’s x=x0​+NSz​N⊤(η−Λp​x0​), with x0​=PH⊤μr​ and η=At⊤​Λr​μr​, written as one matrix for all replicas. Whether a replica’s hard constraints have a common solution depends on its mean, so it is checked per replica, with _from_potential’s residual and tolerance: a contradiction in replica i throws as it would alone. The messages are built with a trusted ExtendedGaussian constructor (the parts are already a normalised representative), so a replica costs a few matrix-vector products and no decomposition.

Anything else takes factor_message per replica: inputs that differ in structure, any number that is not a Float64 (ForwardDiff duals), and a Zygote reverse pass (_within_gradient() is true there through an rrule), since the shared path takes its decompositions on values without the constant-rank derivative terms of LenticulumCore’s. The per-replica loop is a comprehension, as Zygote cannot differentiate writes into a vector (Mycelium’s default loop writes; P6 found that Zygote through it fails).

From message blocks the result is a VectorBlock of the messages: there is no block type for extended Gaussians, so the variable’s side combines them replica by replica.

Measured (test/hard_plates.jl, 300 random instances: 2 or 3 channels of dimension 1 to 3, every kind of input, zero, positive definite and singular noise, 2 to 5 replicas; 278 went through the shared path, 22 were refused by the per-replica path and by the batch alike): the largest relative deviation from factor_message over mean, covariance and flat projector is 9.5⋅10−12. The graph-level oracle is test/extended_plates.jl (a plate of GaussianFactors under hard relations against the dense conditioned Gaussian), which now goes through this method: counted once with an instrumented copy, 261 of its group steps took the shared path and 20 the per-replica one. Timing (one machine, 2026-11-17, a relation on (u,v,t) of dimensions (3,2,3), m=3, Gaussian u of one precision and point v, the message to t; a scratch script, not in the repository, so a rough figure): 0.045 ms against 0.27 ms for 10 replicas, 0.081 against 2.7 ms for 100, 0.44 against 27 ms for 1000.

Review: LP-044 Findings of the review of P6 (the block fallback under Zygote, fixed: it built the block with belief_block, which Zygote cannot take; argument lengths now checked).

Mean-field nodes

With a positive definite noise the factor is LinearConstraintFactor with that noise, and its mean-field message and average energy are that factor’s (LP-044; tested equal on random factors). With zero noise, or a singular one (exact along its null space), they are refused with the reason. The mean-field message expEq​[logf] of the relation is the limit, as ε→0, of LinearConstraintFactor’s with noise εI: N−1(At⊤​dˉ/ε,At⊤​At​/ε) with dˉ=c−∑i=t​Ai​Eq​[xi​], hard along range(At⊤​) at At​xt​=dˉ. The marginal q(xt​)∝p(xt​)μf→t​(xt​) then sits there exactly, whatever the prior says along those directions. Two scalar variables with u−v=0 and priors N(0,1), N(2,1): the update is Eu←Ev, Ev←Eu, so every u=v is a fixed point and the iteration stays where it starts, while the posterior has mean 1. With noise ε the update is Eu←Ev/(1+ε) (and alike), a contraction by (1+ε)−2 per sweep that vanishes as ε→0. The average energy Eq​[−logf] is infinite unless each Ai​xi​ is deterministic under q; its finite part is not the Bethe one either, as the divergent terms (the factor’s 2m​logε, the variables’ 2rankAi​​logε) do not cancel unless ∑i​rankAi​=m. Belief propagation, exact on trees, is the rule for an exact relation; a MeanField() node wants a positive definite noise.

What it does not do

  • Learning: islearnable is false: its own A and c get no gradient. Other factors in its graph do: the coupled replay (free_energy_gradient(...; schedule, ad)) goes through its messages and its free energy under ForwardDiff and Zygote (P4: the SVD of its term is of values, with 21​logdetGG⊤=∑logσi​+tr(G+Δ) and N=N0​−G+ΔN0​ at constant rank, LenticulumCore extended_gaussian.md, “Derivatives”); test/hard_gradient.jl checks it against the gradient of the dense −logZ on random trees. fit_em with the factor in the graph is not tested.
  • A nearly singular noise (an eigenvalue above _eig_tol but tiny, say a relative 10−11) counts as positive definite and goes through LinearConstraintFactor’s formulas, messages included, which invert it: 6 of 20 random trees with such a noise were off by up to 1.6⋅10−6 in F and 4⋅10−5 in the marginals (LP-047 Findings of the review of P5, finding 4; a review script, not a test). A formulation in the residual space would avoid the inverse.
  • Plates whose inputs differ in structure, dual numbers and Zygote take the per-replica path (“Plates”); there is no block type for extended Gaussians.
  • Mean-field messages (MeanField() nodes) of an exact relation: refused (“Mean-field nodes”).
  • Mixture inputs exactly: they are collapsed to their moments (P27), not expanded into one message per component.

Difficulties

The first comparison with LinearConstraintFactor failed in 23 of 149 messages, all with every other input uninformative. The pushforward is then flat in every direction, and projecting Σ with I−DD⊤ left a residue of 10−30, which the relative tolerance took for a real variance: the message came out hard where it should be flat. The extended Gaussian now projects through an orthonormal basis of the complement of D, which is empty in that case, so Σ is exactly zero.

The damped flooding run of one instance (an ill-conditioned A) stops at a residual of 3.8⋅10−10, a rounding floor of the absolute convergence bound (LP-024’s open row), so the test asks for 10−8; its marginals agree to 10−7.

P5’s first tree test failed in one instance: a noise BB⊤ of rank 1 had a zero eigenvalue computed as about 10−16, isposdef accepted it, and the free energy took LinearConstraintFactor’s path while the messages (which split at _eig_tol) had treated that direction as exact. The free energy now decides positive definiteness by the same tolerance. The 512-bit oracle met the same rounding the other way: Q+εQh​Qh⊤​ at ε=10−30 was indefinite, as Q‘s null eigenvalues in Float64 are ±10−17; the oracle rebuilds the noise from its split.

Rank tolerance of the free-energy check (GH-17, 2026-11-17)

The full-row-rank test of the free energy used eps(Float64) on the singular values of G; it now uses _eps_eltype(eltype(S0)), so a Float32 coefficient matrix is judged at Float32 rounding.

Element type (P30)

Q is a type parameter, not a Float64 copy; normalisers, the zero entropy (false) and the shared kernel’s buffers take their inputs’ type, and an absent input is extended(TrivialBelief(), n, T) with T the type of c. P31 also admits Float32 replicas to the shared plate kernel. Test: gaussian.md, “Element type”.

Element type

Computation follows Julia promotion of the parameters, noise, incoming messages and data it reads; data and parameters are not converted to force a result type. Constants and numerical hyperparameters are cast at use.

The shared plate kernel already used typed arrays; its eligibility checks now admit Float32 as well as Float64 inputs and parameters. P31 compares the shared result with each replica separately and checks a plate against dense Gaussian conditioning. Dual inputs continue through the per-replica path. This does not extend the shared kernel to device arrays or new scalar types.

Checks: lib/Lenticulum.jl/test/p31_element_type.jl. The implementation is not a claim of coverage for every mixed precision, derivative, or device configuration.
