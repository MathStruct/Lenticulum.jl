implementation derivation

A separately typed negative ELBO for a normalised finite VP diffusion chain. Analytic Gaussian expectations give an upper bound; sampled expectations return an explicitly different estimate type. The graph’s denoising surrogate is unchanged.

Sources: original finite Gaussian-chain derivation, checked in ../test/bound.jl; code: bound.jl, factor.jl.

Theory (CT-ML wiki): Variational Free Energy · Bayesian Inversion

Normalised model and variational law (2026-10-27, LP-009)

The caller supplies strictly increasing times in (0,1]. Set ai​=αi​/αi−1​, βi​=1−ai2​, α0​=1 and σi2​=1−αi2​. These define a finite model, including its decoder:

q(xi​∣xi−1​)=N(ai​xi−1​,βi​I),p(xT​)=N(0,I),pθ​(xi−1​∣xi​)=N(mi​(xi​),βi​I), mi​(z)=ai​z−βi​εθ​(z,ti​)/σi​​.

For full joint evidence x=x0​, the variational law is q(x1:T​∣x). The negative ELBO includes the terminal KL, every reverse-transition KL, and the Gaussian decoder negative log likelihood, with all constants. Jensen gives

B(x)=−ELBO(x)=−logpθ​(x)+KL(q(x1:T​∣x)∥pθ​(x1:T​∣x))≥−logpθ​(x).

For i>1, the forward posterior has mean αi−1​βi​x/σi2​+ai​σi−12​z/σi2​ and variance βi​σi−12​/σi2​. Its Gaussian KL leaves an expectation only over z∼N(αi​x,σi2​I). For a single-component GaussianMixtureEps, mi​ is affine, so its squared residual expectation is exactly its squared mean plus a covariance trace. No numerical quadrature is called exact.

free_energy_upper_bound(f,x,ps,st;times) returns FreeEnergyUpperBound(value). It supports that analytic single-Gaussian predictor with the default input adapter, a shared time, matching model/predictor schedules and no device transfer. estimate_free_energy_upper_bound supports other predictors using independent Gaussian marginal draws at each time and returns FreeEnergyBoundEstimate(value,standard_error,nsamples). Its expectation is a bound; an individual estimate may fall below the exact negative log evidence. Both APIs require inference-mode state and preserve the supplied state tree, including ProxDM’s separate predictor tree. Neither wrapper is a Real or accepted by scalarise.

Checked scope and difficulties

../test/bound.jl checks the stationary N(0,I) case: its analytic score is −z, all reverse transitions preserve that Gaussian, and the bound equals its closed-form negative log density. Displacing the analytic score’s Gaussian mean gives an affine reverse chain whose marginal is calculated independently; the bound stays above that model’s exact negative log density and the gap decreases as the displacement vanishes. Monte Carlo estimates are compared with the analytic expectation using their standard errors; unsupported schedules, times, dimensions and exact expectations are rejected.

This API evaluates full joint point evidence only. It does not provide partial-channel evidence, a continuous-time likelihood, a bound on (clamp,score), or the entropy of the prox posterior. free_energy and entropy on DiffusionFactor continue to throw with these reasons. The common factor checker still applies only to the existing surrogate message path; its game exemption remains justified. A finite Gaussian decoder and a terminal distribution are essential: the denoising loss alone omits normalisers and is not an ELBO. times changes the model as well as the variational law.
