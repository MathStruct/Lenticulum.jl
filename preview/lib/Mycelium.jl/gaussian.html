Gaussian belief pooling, densities, entropy, damping and public log overlaps live in Mycelium alongside the categorical and mixture rules.

Sources: code: gaussian.jl, ../test/overlap.jl

Theory: Gaussian Belief · Belief Algebra · Bethe Free Energy

Implements: the Mycelium belief interface for LenticulumCore.GaussianBelief.

1. The rules

combineaddition of canonical parameters: exact, associative, total; keeps the improper flag only beside zero precision (gaussian_belief.md, “Declared improper”)

2. History

These methods were in the top-level Lenticulum package with the type, as extensions of Mycelium’s functions. They moved here when the type moved to LenticulumCore (gaussian_belief §2), so that the rules sit beside the categorical and mixture rules in messages. The numerical rules are unchanged; the overlap rule is now public.

Related: messages, free_energy, gaussian_belief

For proper c,d, logZ is log N(μ_c;μ_d,Σ_c+Σ_d). For improper d it is A(c⊙d)−A(c), with its undefined normalisation constant omitted. That constant depends only on d and cancels across mixture components. test/overlap.jl checks the proper formula and differences across two proper first arguments for a rank-one improper likelihood.

Device diagonal messages (2026-10-27)

Gaussian entropy/density use Core’s precision-logdet helper. The CPU path still uses logdet of its Cholesky factor; a device Diagonal uses sum(log, diag). Julia’s generic diagonal logabsdet reduction has no GPU neutral element for dual pairs, which broke ForwardDiff replay even when the counting coefficient was zero. Elementwise positive-diagonal algebra avoids this reduction. Exact/pathwise and terminal score tests with JLArrays compare against CPU and closed-form derivatives in lib/ImplicitLayers.jl/test/device_arrays.jl. Q7 adds the dense precision path described in gaussian_belief. See gradient_couplings for the diagonal replay rules.

Device density/entropy specialisations use only diagonal fields and typed scalar constants. Mixing a native diagonal matrix-product pullback with direct diag field reads produced incompatible Zygote cotangents; the elementwise quadratic form avoids that mix. Generic inv(Diagonal(device)) also scalar-indexes on this stack: Core’s device moment constructor/covariance accessor instead broadcast reciprocals. Their derivatives and score estimates are explicitly tested.

Batch C damping precision (2026-10-28)

The GPUArraysCore extension casts damping weights to diagonal-Gaussian or Dirac payload precision, before broadcasting. A Float64 keyword weight otherwise widens Float32 device messages. CPU damping arithmetic is unchanged. New realised flooding/residual replay tests compare Float32/Float64 diagonal Gaussian tree/loop messages and derivatives against CPU references with scalar indexing disabled. See test/graph_adjoint_devices.jl and gradient_couplings.

Overlaps without cancellation (2026-10-29, LP-019 items 2 and 6)

combine_with_overlap used to return A(c⊙d)−A(c)−A(d). Each log-partition holds a term μ⊤Λμ/2, and at ∣μ∣∼108 the difference loses every digit: two identical N(108,1) gave −4 instead of −21​log4π, and a mixture’s product weights shifted by 108 became [0.5, 0.5] instead of [0.5312, 0.4688]. Proper pairs now use the convolution logN(μc​−μd​;0,Σc​+Σd​), which depends only on the difference of the means. For an improper d the value is still log∫pc​(x)exp(η⊤x−x⊤Λx/2)dx (the convention overlap.jl tests), expanded about μc​. That value is genuinely of size η⊤μc​, which is harmless when the mixture weights are normalised.

Translation invariance holds until the canonical form itself fails: beyond ∣μ∣∼1012, μ=Λ−1η is recovered only to ∼10−16∣μ∣, which no overlap formula repairs.

The rule needs a proper first argument. _mixture_product now puts a proper Gaussian first, whichever side of the product it came from, so pooling a mixture with an improper site no longer depends on the factor insertion order. Tested in test/lp019.jl.

After review (2026-10-30)

Codex’s review of the fix found four gaps, now closed: the proper case called inv on a Cholesky factor (no Zygote rule) and scalar-indexed diagonal device arrays, so it now goes through belief_cov and LenticulumCore’s _precision_factor/_solve/_logdet; finite covariances near floatmax overflowed when added, so Σc​+Σd​ is scaled by its largest diagonal entry; and an improper site far from the origin left log masses of size 1016, so mixture products re-centre each improper site at one reference point (the first proper component’s mean on the other side; _overlap(c, d, ref)) and normalise by subtracting the maximum first. combine_with_overlap(c, d) itself keeps the unshifted convention tested in overlap.jl.

Second review (2026-10-30): the proper case scales per coordinate, D−1(Σc​+Σd​)D−1 with D=diag(max(Σc,ii​,Σd,ii​)​) (_scaled_sum, diagonal-preserving, so huge and tiny variances coexist and device/Zygote paths broadcast); and mixture products re-centre improper sites only when the other side has exactly one, since the dropped constant differs between sites.

Expectation propagation (2026-11-03)

The Gaussian pieces of the ExpectationPropagationRule() (passing.md):

  • ep_project(m::MixtureBelief{<:GaussianBelief}): the total mean μ=∑k​wk​μk​ and covariance ∑k​wk​(Σk​+(μk​−μ)(μk​−μ)⊤), as Lenticulum’s moment_match, but returning nothing instead of throwing when a component is not proper or the covariance is not positive definite: the rule then skips the update.
  • ep_divide(p, c): (ηp​−ηc​,Λp​−Λc​), declared improper unless the difference is positive definite. EP sites are often not: on the clutter problem a site’s precision is negative for an outlier the cavity already explains.
  • _indefinite(b): a precision with an eigenvalue below −16dεmax∣λ∣; an EP node skips an update whose factor would receive such a cavity, since the factors take positive semidefinite inputs only.

Products of improper components (2026-11-03)

combine_with_overlap(c, d) used to require a proper c. When both are improper, unnormalised exponentials exp(η⊤x−21​x⊤Λx) as a likelihood mixture carries them, the product is exact: the exponentials multiply, (ηc​+ηd​,Λc​+Λd​), and there is no overlap integral to take. If the sum is proper it is normalised and its mass 21​η⊤Λ−1η+2d​log2π−21​logdetΛ moves into logZ; otherwise it stays an unnormalised exponential with logZ=0. With a re-centring point ref (LP-019) the same constant of d is dropped as in the proper case, d evaluated at ref. Two mixtures of likelihoods, the messages of two data points to one variable, can now be pooled; exact belief propagation on the clutter problem needs it (test/mixture_rules.jl, which also checks the formula on a 1-d pair and the improper case).

The normalisation subtracts no log-partitions from each other, but it does compute 21​η⊤Λ−1η directly, which is large when the product is far from the origin; the LP-019 care for proper pairs (the convolution form) has no counterpart here.

Extended Gaussians (2026-11-10)

combine takes LenticulumCore.ExtendedGaussians (lib/LenticulumCore.jl/src/extended_gaussian.md): two of them, or one and a GaussianBelief, combine by the exact product (_conjoin, beside the type), which stacks hard constraints, adds precisions on what they leave free, and keeps the directions flat in both. A DiracBelief still dominates, as against a Gaussian, but is checked first against the hard constraints of the other side: a clamp that violates one is an ArgumentError, like two unequal clamps. The result of a product with an extended Gaussian is an extended Gaussian, also when both inputs were representable in canonical form.

The store takes them (2026-11-11). belief_distance compares μ, Σ and the projector onto the flat directions (independent of their basis); against a GaussianBelief, DiracBelief or TrivialBelief it compares the other’s extended form, so a message that changes kind converges. damp is the geometric mixture LenticulumCore._damp_extended: the canonical rule on Gaussian directions, gradual on directions flat on one side, the weighted average of two hard values. Since 2026-11-12 (P1) this holds for a mixed pair too, an extended Gaussian and a GaussianBelief in either order: the GaussianBelief is damped as its extended form. Before, can_damp had no method for the pair, and damp returned the new message undamped without a sign, which happens whenever a likelihood changes form between sweeps (flat while an input is missing, proper once it arrives). Against a GaussianBelief of dual numbers (ForwardDiff), combine, belief_distance and damp take the extended side in canonical form when it has no hard directions; with hard directions there is no canonical form, and since P4 (2026-11-16) the dual side is taken in its extended form, which moves at constant rank (LenticulumCore extended_gaussian.md, “Derivatives”). Until P4 that pair was an ArgumentError (can_damp was false). Before, combine threw a MethodError there, inside infer!, on a variable that hears a dual flagged message and a plain extended one (LP-028 Findings of the review of P1, F1). test/forms_crossing.jl checks combine, belief_distance and damp on 150 random pairs of proper, flagged and zero-precision beliefs, each side in either form, against the operation with both sides in canonical form (3 003 checks); the damping checks fail against the code before the fix; and the dual pairs on 60 random instances (1 080 checks). Not checked: the distance near the rank threshold. A message whose precision crosses it between sweeps changes form, and its distance in extended form then counts the change of the flat subspace (up to 1) and the large variance, where the canonical distance would be small. This is expected from the definitions, not measured on a run. HardConstraintFactor (Lenticulum) emits extended Gaussians under any schedule. Tests: test/runtests.jl, “combine with extended Gaussians” and “the message store takes extended Gaussians”.

Entropy (2026-11-11). variable_entropy of an extended Gaussian is LenticulumCore.support_entropy: the entropy relative to Lebesgue measure on the belief’s support, 0 for a point, as for a DiracBelief. A variable that a hard constraint fixes in some directions therefore has a finite counting term, and flat directions (an improper marginal) throw. On trees with a hard relation the Bethe free energy is then −logZ (Lenticulum test/hard_free_energy.jl; the convention is in lib/LenticulumCore.jl/src/extended_gaussian.md, “Entropy”).

Mixtures meeting extended Gaussians (2026-11-17, P27)

A SwitchingConstraintFactor on a variable that a HardConstraintFactor also reaches sends a mixture of Gaussians where the relation sends an extended Gaussian with hard directions. The mixture product (_mixture_product, messages.jl) needs combine_with_overlap for the pair, and had none: ArgumentError: no product rule for mixture components.

The overlap. Let d fix H⊤x=h (its hard directions H, orthonormal) and be Gaussian or flat on its support S, written x=x0​+Us with U an orthonormal basis of H⊥ (LenticulumCore _hard_reduction), where its Gaussian part is the canonical pair (ηs​,Λs​). The weight of a component c is

Z=∫S​pc​(x)pd​(x)dσ(x),

with σ Lebesgue measure on S in the coordinates s. It is the limit of ∫pc​(x)N(x;μd​,Σd​+εI)dx as ε→0, and finite: for d flat nowhere it is Ex∼d​[pc​(x)], an expectation under a probability measure on S, so weights of components against supports of any dimension compare. A proper c factors on S into the density of its marginal H⊤x at h and its conditional given H⊤x=h, whose canonical form in s is (U⊤(ηc​−Λc​x0​),U⊤Λc​U):

logZ=logN(h;H⊤μc​,H⊤Σc​H)+log∫cs​(s)ds​(s)ds.

The second term is the Gaussian overlap above (_overlap), exact for d proper on S and up to a constant of d alone when d is flat somewhere (as for an improper Gaussian site: the weights of one mixture against the one site are right). Neither term subtracts large log-partitions. An improper c (an unnormalised exponential) has no marginal; its term is its value at x0​ plus the overlap of its restriction. Without hard directions the rule is the Gaussian pair’s. Two extended Gaussians that both have hard directions are refused: two laws on lower-dimensional supports meet in a set of measure zero for both, and their overlap is not finite. Code: _extended_overlap.

Expectation propagation. With ExpectationPropagationRule() on the switching node the cavity is extended, and the tilted belief (cavity × mixture) a mixture of extended Gaussians that share the cavity’s hard directions. ep_project of it is the extended Gaussian of the total moments, zero variance along those directions, so they stay hard (nothing if a component is flat somewhere). ep_divide(p, cavity) is defined on the cavity’s support only, where both are Gaussian with log densities η⊤x−21​x⊤Λx+const for their canonical parts (zero precision along hard and flat directions): the site is the GaussianBelief (ηp​−ηc​,Λp​−Λc​), flat along the hard directions, declared improper, and as any Gaussian site possibly indefinite. A projection whose hard directions or hard values differ from the cavity’s gives nothing (the update is skipped; the values were not checked before the review, LP-046 Findings of the review of P27, F1).

An indefinite Gaussian times an extended one. combine(a::GaussianBelief, b::ExtendedGaussian) went through extended(a), which refuses a negative direction, so the site above could not meet the cavity again. For an indefinite a (_indefinite) the product is now the product of potentials [Hb⊤​x=Hb⊤​μb​]⋅exp((ηa​+ηb​)⊤x−21​x⊤(Λa​+Λb​)x) through LenticulumCore _from_potential (_potential_conjoin). It checks that the summed precision is positive semidefinite on what b leaves free, since _psd_split would count a negative eigenvalue as a zero one and return a flat direction where the product is not normalisable. It also checks that nothing is left along a direction where the precision is zero. Other Gaussians take the old path unchanged.

Lenticulum’s side. The messages that leave the variable carry the mixture into the relation factors: HardConstraintFactor refused a mixture input, and LinearConstraintFactor collapsed only Gaussian mixtures. Both now collapse a mixture, also of extended components, to its total moments (moment_match), as they collapse Gaussian mixtures (assumed-density filtering, Lenticulum gaussian.md, “Mixture inputs”).

Tests. test/overlap.jl, “P27: combine_with_overlap of a Gaussian and an extended Gaussian”: 60 random instances (2 to 4 dimensions, 1 to n hard directions, flat directions, proper and improper components) against the closed form log∫cdd over x=μd​+Gz+Ft (z standard normal, t Lebesgue), to 10−9, plus a translation by 106, the point, the refusal and the soft case (383 checks). “P27: expectation propagation with an extended cavity”: combine(ep_divide(p, cavity), cavity) gives p back on 40 random instances, indefinite sites among them, and the product of an indefinite Gaussian with a hard relation against the dense solve on the support, and the refusal of F1 (285 checks). Lenticulum test/mixture_extended.jl: switching factor and hard relation on one variable, in plates, under both schedules and both rules, against enumeration of the outcome (1152 checks, largest error 1.4×10−11).

What it does not do. Two extended components with hard directions: refused (above). No re-centring for an improper component far from the origin (LP-019’s reference point is used only for an improper Gaussian site), so it may lose digits there; not measured. A proper one does not: a translation of both sides by 106 changes logZ by less than 10−7 (tested). Derivatives (ForwardDiff, Zygote) through the new overlap and through _potential_conjoin are not tested. The Bethe free energy of a graph whose variable marginal is a mixture has no variable_entropy (not for Gaussian mixtures either: a mixture’s entropy has no closed form), so scalar_free_energy refuses it under BeliefPropagation(); under ExpectationPropagationRule() the marginal is the projection and it runs (probe, not a test).

Difficulties. The first oracle, the limit Σd​+εHH⊤ and L⋅FF⊤, agreed only to 10−6 (truncation at large ε, rounding at small); the closed form over x=μd​+Gz+Ft replaced it. The silent flat direction of _potential_conjoin was found by its refusal test.

Mixture reduction (2026-11-12, P17)

reduce_mixture(m; max_components, prune) and reduce_message(::MixtureBelief{<:GaussianBelief}, M), the message of a MixtureReduction(M) node, are defined here. They were in Lenticulum’s beliefs.jl, where the reduce_message method was type piracy (Mycelium’s function on LenticulumCore’s types), and Mycelium without Lenticulum refused to reduce a Gaussian mixture. The code is unchanged; Lenticulum re-exports reduce_mixture. The theory (Runnalls’ bound on the KL divergence of a merge, which keeps the total mean and covariance) and the refusal of improper components over the budget are in Lenticulum’s beliefs.md, “The reduction of a message”.

Tested in test/mixture_reduction.jl with Mycelium and LenticulumCore only: on 30 random mixtures of 2–6 components, every budget from 1 to K + 1 gives the mixture unchanged within the budget, the moment-matched Gaussian at one, and otherwise the requested number of components with the total mean and covariance kept to 10−10. Mistral compared the moved functions with the code before the move on 40 random mixtures and found them identical (LP-029 Findings of the review of P17 and P18).

Damping and the improper flag (GH-18, 2026-11-17)

_damp combined the metadata as a.improper && b.improper, so two improper sites with complementary nullspaces — whose weighted precision sum is positive definite — kept the result improper and belief_cov refused it. The flag of a damped pair is now decided from the combined support (_damped_improper): when both sides are flagged, the result is proper only if each precision is numerically rank-deficient (the flag’s claim, visible in the spectrum) and the sum is full rank; coincident nullspaces, a flag contradicted by a full-rank side, and a singular sum stay improper, and the endpoints reduce to the flagged side’s own. The rank is computed on values (stop_gradient) with the tolerance of the decomposition’s element type (_numerical_rank), so a Float32 precision is judged at eps(Float32). The device _damp (diagonal precisions) applies the same rule through reductions only. Tests: test/runtests.jl, “GH-18” (complementary, coincident, contradicted flags, endpoints, dense and diagonal precisions, Float32), and test/device_improper.jl under LENTICULUM_DEVICE_TESTS=true.

Element type

P32 removes Float64 normalisers from Gaussian log densities and overlap weights, including improper/improper and Gaussian/hard-extended products. Each log(2π) is evaluated with LenticulumCore._log2π(T) in the promoted computation’s type; an absent reference shift is a typed zero. Inputs are not narrowed or converted to a fixed precision: mixed inputs promote. The Float64 arithmetic order is retained. test/element_type.jl checks proper overlaps against their closed form and exercises log densities, improper products and a hard point. Comparison tolerances, rank decisions and mixture reduction policy are unchanged; these are not energy or message storage types. Extended/extended algebra belongs to LenticulumCore and is not changed here.

Device arrays (Q7)

Mixture projection reads one weight per host-side component and constructs the total covariance on the component backend. Gaussian projection factors that covariance with the shared SPD routine.

Q20 device message paths

Mixture reduction uses host weights for pruning and pair selection, keeps the component moments and covariance factorizations on the device, and uploads the final weights. EP projection downloads its normalised weights once for the component loop and keeps the projected moments on the device.

EP site division keeps the device precision on the device for the definiteness check.

belief_distance of two extended Gaussians moves both to a common device when one is on the host (the extended form of a TrivialBelief, met in a mean-field sweep on CUDA; LP-095).
