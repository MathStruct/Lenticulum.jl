Scope

Two message rules beside BeliefPropagation() and MeanField(), chosen per node with factor!(b, name, f; rule), for factors whose belief-propagation message leaves the target’s family (a mixture of Gaussians). Expectation propagation keeps every message in the family; mixture reduction keeps mixtures within a budget. Cluster nodes (§5 of the design note) are out of scope.

The contract

§1. ExpectationPropagationRule(). The node computes the belief-propagation message mt​ from its cavities, as a BeliefPropagation() node does. If mt​ is not a MixtureBelief it is sent. Otherwise, with πt​ the target’s cavity, it sends ep_message(m_t, π_t) =proj[πt​mt​]/πt​: ep_project matches moments (a Gaussian mixture: total mean and covariance), and ep_divide subtracts canonical parameters, declaring the site improper unless its precision is positive definite. An uninformative cavity leaves mt​ as the tilted belief; a point cavity sends mt​ unchanged. The update is skipped, the edge keeping its message, when the cavity is a Gaussian that is not proper, when the tilted belief has a component that is not proper, or when an input to the factor has a negative direction. The first skip of a run warns. The name avoids Lenticulum’s ExpectationPropagation, the inversion method of LikelihoodFactor.

§2. MixtureReduction(M). The node computes mixture_message(factor, target, polarity, inputs, prior, ps, st) when an input is a MixtureBelief (else factor_message) and sends reduce_message(message, M). mixture_message is the exact message with mixture inputs expanded into their components; a factor without a method refuses. reduce_message returns a belief that is not a mixture, or has at most M components, unchanged; Lenticulum reduces a Gaussian mixture with Runnalls’ merge and returns the component when one is left; a mixture with an improper component over the budget is refused. MixtureReduction(0) is refused.

§3. Implementations. Lenticulum implements mixture_message for SwitchingConstraintFactor (to a continuous channel and to the outcome) and LinearConstraintFactor, with mixture inputs of proper Gaussian components and the other inputs as factor_message takes them, flat directions included (switching.md §9).

§4. Schedules. One undamped TreeSchedule sweep is reported exact only when every node is a belief-propagation or mixture-reduction node; a graph with an expectation-propagation or mean-field node is iterated. Plate sweeps group belief-propagation and (on message blocks only) expectation-propagation nodes, L-SPEC-006 Plates §8; mixture-reduction nodes go edge by edge.

§5. Free energy. An expectation-propagation node is counted as a belief-propagation node: its local_free_energy at its incoming messages, and its edges in the counting numbers. The default free energy and gradient drivers accept it; the drivers that differentiate through inference and the residual-moment recorder refuse it. Every energy path refuses a mixture-reduction node.

§6. Products of improper components. combine_with_overlap of two improper Gaussians (two likelihood components) is their exact product; normalised with its mass in logZ when proper, with logZ=0 otherwise.

Checked by

test/mixture_rules.jl:

  • the clutter problem (D=1, w=0.5, variances 100 and 10, data from the model): belief propagation’s 2N-component marginal against enumeration to 10−12; the rule at the fixed point of a hand-written Minka loop to 10−10, on a tree and on a sequential schedule; at N=12 within 6⋅10−4 of the exact mean and 2% of the variance; at N=4 the same oscillation as the hand loop;
  • the rule’s pieces: a message in its family passes, an improper cavity skips, a point cavity passes, the site times the cavity has the tilted moments, an indefinite site is declared improper, a Gaussian graph gives belief propagation’s marginals;
  • a location-mixture likelihood on EP nodes: the gradient at fixed messages against central differences of the re-converged free energy, 10−7 relative; the implicit adjoint refused;
  • mixture_message against pointwise oracles to 10−12 (both factors, both kinds of target), with a flat input against a prior of variance 108 to 10−6, equal to factor_message without mixture inputs, and its refusals;
  • a switching linear chain (T=5, K=2) against enumeration: errors 0.16, 0.011, 9.6⋅10−4, 1.0⋅10−4 and 1.6⋅10−15 for budgets 1, 2, 4, 8, 16; belief propagation equal to budget 2; the free energy refused; reduce_message’s cases;
  • the product of two improper components against its closed form.

test/plates.jl checks the switching plates of L-SPEC-006 §7.

Open

  • Mixture-reduction nodes in plates run edge by edge (their component counts differ between replicas); EP plates feeding a linear factor go replica by replica there (L-SPEC-006 §8.4).
  • No free energy for reduction nodes, and none for belief-propagation graphs with Gaussian-mixture marginals (no closed-form entropy).
  • Expanded mixture inputs must have proper components.
  • The projection is onto Gaussians only (and categoricals pass as they are); other families would add an ep_project method.
