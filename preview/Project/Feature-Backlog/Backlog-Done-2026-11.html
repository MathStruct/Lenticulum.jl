2026-11-16

P4, extended-Gaussian inputs in plates (tested earlier the same day, no code change) and under AD: the coupled replay (free_energy_gradient(...; schedule, ad), ExactCoupling) through a HardConstraintFactor runs under ForwardDiff and Zygote. A first-order rank change (L⊤ΔR=0) is refused under ForwardDiff, nested duals refused; Zygote gets no rank check. _independent_join builds without mutation; Mycelium’s combine, belief_distance and _damp of an extended and a dual Gaussian take the extended path when there are hard directions; Lenticulum’s _range_complement (float) and _accumulate became Zygote-safe on the way (extended_gaussian “Derivatives (P4, 2026-11-16)”, hard_constraint). Tests: LenticulumCore test/extended_gaussian_ad.jl (pushforward, preimage, product, support entropy, hard projector, canonical part and the conversion from an improper Gaussian, with moving subspaces, ForwardDiff and Zygote against central differences; beliefs with no Gaussian direction; refusals), Lenticulum test/hard_gradient.jl (12 random trees with a hard relation, the replay’s gradient under ForwardDiff and Zygote against ForwardDiff of the dense −logZ, to 1e-8), the replay @test_broken of test/extended_plates.jl, Mycelium test/forms_crossing.jl (a hard × dual pair, values and central differences). Review: LP-043 Findings of the review of P4 (one finding, fixed). Left: P27 (mixture × extended); Zygote has no rank check; second derivatives refused.

Review: LP-042 Findings of the review of P25 (one finding, fixed). Left: a second-order rank change gets the constant-rank derivative; Zygote not covered.

Tests: Lenticulum test/regime_plate.jl, random constant-rank J(θ) (half with repeated eigenvalues) through _psd_logdet, _psd_solve, _psd_inv against central differences, a rank change refused, random regime factors with flat x (ForwardDiff against central differences, Zygote against ForwardDiff), P24’s former @test_broken. Review: LP-041 Findings of the review of P26.

F3, the ridge residual: ridge_residual(m, z, ps, st; codim) in VariationalDiffusion (answers.jl, implicit §5) is the first-order distance from a point to the ridge of the smoothed density, ∥VΛ−1V⊤g∥ with V,Λ the codim largest eigenpairs of the field Jacobian’s symmetric part, Inf when a normal curvature is not positive; for an answer the codimension defaults to its number of free coordinates. query_report reports it with DenseSolve and flags :off_ridge when it is Inf or above ridge_tol (report). Tests: exact against a rotated Gaussian (the distance to the major axis, invariant in λ), the ring (on it within 2⋅10−3, off it 1.3−R′; at the top the round trip rejects a true root, the ridge residual does not), a non-symmetric linear field and a saddle; mutations of the formula fail them. Reviewed by Mistral (LP-033 Findings of the review of the ridge residual): no wrong result, four missing tests, added. Measured on the calibration benchmark (calibration.md “Remedies”, point 4): at the low levels it keeps every matched root and every off-domain root goes, where the round trip loses recall (0.86 to 0.74 on the circle, 0.52 to 0.12 on the lemniscate); it removes fewer spurious roots than coverage, but needs no data and costs no recall at the lemniscate’s crossing.

2026-11-11

The improper flag stays (decided 2026-11-11, with Daniel). The former row “retire the flag” would have moved the last canonical-form messages to ExtendedGaussian, but that type cannot go where they are: it holds only AbstractFloat values, and its flat directions come from eigendecompositions that ForwardDiff’s dual numbers have no method for; plate blocks keep one η matrix with a shared Λ, which a basis of flat directions per replica would break; and the weights of a mixture of likelihoods rest on the constants of the canonical form, which _overlap re-centres (LP-019). So the canonical form with the flag is the form for AD, batches and mixtures, and ExtendedGaussian the exact host form of linear relations, the only one with hard directions. What is left is P1, the tests where the two meet. The former first row, the ExtendedGaussian type with exact combine, marginalisation and pushforward, is done with it (below and 2026-11-10).

The backlog got ids and a needs column, and the dependency graph in Feature Backlog.

P7, the free energy and the state tree: the main cost was _check_tied_state, which counted the factors of every key on every call (O(keys × factors)), not the write-back of the state that LP-024 suspected. It is linear now, and a factor’s state is written back only when it changed. The free energy is 1.5–2.3 times faster (42.9 instead of 95.2 ms at 2 401 factors), a sweep unchanged; both stay quadratic until I5 (lib/Mycelium.jl/src/passing.md, “The state tree”; bench/state_scaling.jl; lib/Mycelium.jl/test/state_tree.jl).

An external baseline: Benchmark against RxInfer (bench/rxinfer_baseline.jl). A Kalman smoother: both exact to 10−10; Lenticulum within a factor 1.7 up to T=100, 4.3–5.8 times slower at 1 000–2 000. A mixture of regressions: fit_em is exact EM, 9–10 times slower than RxInfer’s VMP, which is slightly worse in likelihood.

The message store takes extended Gaussians (belief_distance on moments and the flat projector; damp as the geometric mixture). HardConstraintFactor: the linear relation imposed exactly, its messages preimage(pushforward(…)) of extended Gaussians, tested against LinearConstraintFactor with noise, against the Gaussian conditioned on the equality, and in graphs under tree and damped flooding schedules (hard_constraint). Messages only; no free energy.

Every relation factor takes an ExtendedGaussian input, so a HardConstraintFactor can share a variable with them; before, it threw a MethodError. Tested against HardConstraintFactor with the same noise on inputs mixing flat, hard and Gaussian directions, and in a tree against the dense conditioned Gaussian (gaussian “Extended Gaussian inputs”).

The free energy of extended Gaussians: support_entropy (relative to Lebesgue measure on the belief’s support, 0 for a point) as variable_entropy, and HardConstraintFactor’s term 21​logdetG~G~⊤−HR​(bf​), the finite limit of the noisy relation’s. Smooth factors substitute hard inputs out. On 40 random trees the Bethe free energy is −logZ (hard_constraint “Free energy”, extended_gaussian “Entropy”).

The relation factors emit an ExtendedGaussian where they declared a message improper (GaussianFactor and NonlinearFactor backwards, LinearConstraintFactor); the suites needed only two tests changed, which read the old representation (gaussian “Improper messages as extended Gaussians”).
