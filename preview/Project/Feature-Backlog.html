11 Nov 202622 min read

overview

The features Lenticulum.jl should get, in one list: what each is for, where it was asked for, what measurement motivates it, and how far it is. A feature is added here when a specification, a problem, a review or a measurement asks for it, and moved to “done” with the commit that implemented it. Priorities are proposals until Daniel sets them.

Sources: original to this repository; the open lists of Specifications and Problem Log, LP-023 Findings of the external reviews of 2026-11-04.

Status: planned (specified or asked for, not started), proposed (an idea with a reason, no specification), partial, done. Priority: high (it limits something measured now), medium, low.

Underdamped Langevin dynamics on the Bethe free energy: beliefs b (with a mass, e.g. Fisher) get a momentum p, with b˙=p, p˙​=−∇F(b)−γp+2γT​ξ. At T=0 the friction γ brakes H=F+21​∥p∥2 at every step (H˙=−γ∥p∥2), a Lyapunov function that a BP sweep lacks; with T annealed to 0 it could also hop between fixed points. An idea, untested. To settle: must it descend ∇F directly (a BP sweep is not a gradient of F, so momentum on messages may amplify oscillation); how to stay on the constraints (normalised, consistent beliefs: mirror or natural coordinates); Gaussian Bethe energies can be unbounded below on loops (from memory, to check). Test: iterations and answers on the loopy graphs that oscillate today, against damped BP and the exact marginals. Interface (checked 2026-11-06): a schedule defined outside the package plugs in through AbstractSchedule and a sweep! method, and infer! runs it (scratch test on the divider graph: at β=0 it reproduces flooding exactly; the sweep count it gave is not recorded, bench/bp_spectrum.jl measures 326 sweeps to an error of 1e-10). Missing, all additive: the gradient of F in the beliefs (free_energy_gradient is for parameters), coordinates and a retraction for beliefs, and a state holding primal beliefs (off a fixed point the store’s messages do not give consistent beliefs). Naive heavy ball on BP messages failed there for every β≥0.3 tried (negative precisions; with momentum on η only, NaN)proposedlowDaniel, 2026-11-06loopy BP has no convergence guarantee and damping only sometimes cures oscillation (Loopy Message Passing); Momentum exists for parameters but nothing for beliefs
Accelerated mirror descent on the Bethe free energy: descend F in the beliefs with the entropy (log-partition) as the mirror map, so every iterate is a valid belief by construction (for exponential families, mirror descent in the mean parameters is a natural-gradient step in the natural ones: Raskutti & Mukherjee, arXiv:1310.7780), plus momentum (accelerated mirror descent: Krichene, Bayen & Bartlett, NeurIPS 2015, metadata to check; stochastic version with averaging, the analogue of the Langevin row: Krichene & Bartlett, arXiv:1707.06219)proposedlowDaniel, 2026-11-07the failure of naive momentum in the row above was an invalid belief (a negative precision); a mirror map rules that out. Out-of-core graphs (planned subrepository)

A graph too big for memory, kept in a store and loaded where a query reaches. It is planned as its own package, lib/MyceliumStore.jl (working name), on top of Mycelium. Daniel, 2026-11-05: not only for language models but also for heterogeneous multi-omics or streaming time series (markets, sensors). Most of the computation can be avoided in most cases, while particular data is still needed many times.
