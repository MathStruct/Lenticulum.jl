Status: resolved (2026-11-17). The review of P3 (a GaussianFactor with no input on x sends the exact forward message) found no defect in the new message or polarity. One finding was right: a mixture whose components all carry no information threw in moment_match, older than P3 but named by its requirement; fixed with a regression test. Related: Feature Backlog (P3), gaussian “No input on x”.

Sources: a review of the uncommitted P3 change by Mistral (vibe --agent review, Mistral Large 4, --max-price 3, $0 recorded), in the helper worktree Lenticulum.jl-mistral at 0ca25bcd with the diff applied. The first run crashed on its first command inside vibe (a validation error of its own, stdout-run1-crash.md); the second ran to its 100-turn limit. The brief, the answer, the probes and their outputs are in ../Lenticulum.jl-Review-2026-11-17/p3/. code: lib/Lenticulum.jl/src/gaussian.jl; test: lib/Lenticulum.jl/test/p3_no_input.jl.

Problem. The requirement given to the reviewer: the forward message with no information on x (no entry, nothing, TrivialBelief(), zero precision, everywhere flat, a mixture that collapses to one of those) is the limit of x∼N(0,τI), τ→∞, for every shape and rank of A, under factor_message, _gradient_message and ForwardDiff; the backward message stays TrivialBelief(); informative inputs are unchanged; the new polarity breaks nothing that enumerates polarities.

The answer file records probe 1 and its diagnostics; probes 2, 3 and 3b are not in it. The maintainer ran them (outputs probe2.out, probe3.out, probe3b.out).
