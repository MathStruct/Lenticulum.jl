LP-019 Defects found in the review of the core packages

30 Oct 202615 min read

  • open-problem

open-problem

Status: resolved (2026-10-29) by Claude; reviewed by Codex five times (2026-10-30); all follow-ups fixed by Claude, limitations recorded. Related: LP-018 Defects found in the review of the older packages, Messages are Inversions, DEQ as a Relation, The Equilibrium Family, Bethe Free Energy.

Problem. Codex ran a read-only review (2026-10-29) of Mycelium, LenticulumCore (with its extensions), ImplicitLayers and the vault notes on factor graphs and the equilibrium family. It reported 12 defects. Claude reran every reproducer before recording it here and got the same numbers. The reproducers are scratch scripts outside the repository (~/.julia/lenticulum-runs/review-core/). Each fix must therefore add a regression test with the failing input below.

Code defects, most severe first:

  1. A relay changes the Bethe free energy (lib/Mycelium.jl/src/factors.jl, local_free_energy(::RelayFactor, …)). A categorical prior [0.3, 0.7] on one variable has F=0. Relaying it to a second variable leaves the model and the marginals unchanged but gives F=0.6108643, which is H(p). The relay is an equality factor: its energy is zero, but its joint belief p(a)δ(a=b) has entropy H(p), and the zero contribution leaves the extra degree of the relayed variable uncancelled. The contribution should be −H(q), with q the relayed belief (relative to the measure on the diagonal).
  2. Pooling a mixture with an improper Gaussian depends on operand order (src/messages.jl, _mixture_product; src/gaussian.jl, combine_with_overlap). Components N(-1, 1), N(1, 1) with equal weights and a site η = [0.3], Λ = [0] give weights [0.3543, 0.6457] mixture-first. Site-first throws “requires a proper first Gaussian belief”. The order follows the factor insertion order, so the same graph built in a different order fails.
  3. Gaussian validation reads one triangle (lib/LenticulumCore.jl/src/gaussian_belief.jl, _chol, _precision_isproper). Covariance [1 0.2; 10 1] is accepted and becomes [1 0.2; 0.2 1]. As a precision it passes isproper, although its symmetric part has eigenvalues −4.1 and 6.1. LP-018 item 7 fixed the same defect in the top-level constructors.
  4. Finite differences divide by the requested step, not the step taken (lib/ImplicitLayers.jl/src/solve.jl, fd_jacobian; src/flow.jl, _divergence). The increment is rounded to the element type, but the quotient uses the unrounded h. fd_jacobian(identity, Float32[1]) returns 1.1920929, and the RK4 log-determinant of u′=u on [0,1] (50 steps) is 0.9758 instead of 1. In Float16 both return 0.
  5. A damped tree schedule claims exact convergence (lib/Mycelium.jl/src/passing.jl, propagate!). Warm-start a unary Gaussian source at mean 0, change its parameter to mean 10, and propagate with damping = 0.5. The marginal mean is 5, but the report says converged, residual 0, “one sweep is exact”. The fixed point is 10.
  6. Gaussian overlaps cancel catastrophically (src/gaussian.jl, combine_with_overlap). The log-partition difference loses everything at large means: two identical N(1e8, 1) give −4 instead of −21​log4π=−1.2655. Shifting a two-component mixture and its evidence by 1e8 changes the product weights from [0.5312, 0.4688] to [0.5, 0.5]; at a shift of 1e6 they are still correct.
  7. The weight-normalisation pullback overflows (lib/LenticulumCore.jl/ext/LenticulumCoreChainRulesCoreExt.jl). At equal weights the gradient of sum(CategoricalBelief(w).logp) is exactly 0. At w = [1e-308, 1e-308] it is [-Inf, -Inf], at [1e-320, 1e-320] [NaN, NaN]; 1e-307 is still fine.
  8. Scalar particles come back from SampleList as zero-dimensional arrays (ext/LenticulumCoreExponentialFamilyExt.jl, as_belief(::SampleList)). SampleBelief([1, 3], [0.5, 0.5]) round-trips to samples [fill(1), fill(3)]. Pooling with categorical probabilities [0.2, 0.3, 0.5] gives [2/7, 5/7] before and throws after.
  9. A residual schedule with no budget certifies convergence (src/passing.jl, sweep!(…, ::ResidualSchedule, …)). With maxtasks = 0 or -1 and a unary DataFactor(:x, 7), no message is computed; the marginal is TrivialBelief() and the report says converged, residual 0. The expected marginal is DiracBelief(7).
  10. Squared residuals underflow in the root solvers (lib/ImplicitLayers.jl/src/solve.jl, _norm). sqrt(sum(abs2, v)) returns 0 for v = [1e-200]. With a constant residual [1e-200] and tol = 1e-210, Picard, Broyden and limited Broyden all report convergence at iteration 0, and there is no root.

Wrong claims in notes:

  1. “A factor → variable message is the inversion cπ′​” (Messages are Inversions; also DEQ as a Relation §5). Mycelium’s messages are likelihood sites; the inversion is the posterior. For a prior N(0,1) and an observation y=2 with unit noise the outgoing message is N(2,1) and invert returns N(1,0.5). Pooling the note’s message with the prior gives N(2/3,1/3), which is wrong.
  2. “A singular I−∂z​g means the relation branches” (DEQ as a Relation §4, The Equilibrium Family §4). For g(z,x)=z−z3+x the relation is z3=x, with the unique real root 3x​. At x=z=0, I−∂z​g=0 and ift_sensitivity throws. A singular Jacobian means the implicit function theorem’s sufficient condition fails (here: the root is not differentiable in x); branching has to be shown separately.

Found while verifying the fixes (Claude, 2026-10-29):

  1. LP-018 item 7 rejects covariances that are symmetric up to rounding (src/constraint.jl, src/nonlinear.jl). The LP-018 fix tests issymmetric(Q), which is exact. AlgebraicFactors builds Q=JSJ⊤+λΘΘ⊤, which rounds asymmetrically, and its “Polynomial degree-one messages and lens contract” testset errors with “noise covariance must be symmetric”. LP-018’s verification ran five suites, not AlgebraicFactors, so this reached master with commit 5513f457.

Checked and found correct (Codex, 2026-10-29):

  • Mycelium: 546 existing assertions; 458 new ones over six Gaussian trees (different roots; tree, flooding and residual schedules) agreeing with dense references in means, variances, cavity exclusion, counting numbers and Bethe free energy; dense and Krylov implicit graph gradients.
  • LenticulumCore: 234 assertions on energy algebra, scalarisation, Sampson localisation, belief construction and lazy polarity enumeration.
  • ImplicitLayers: 544 assertions on linear DEQ solves in both directions, sensitivities, games, anchors, energy-error checks and Euler/RK4 convergence orders.

Documented limits (dropped categorical labels, legacy state threading) were excluded as defects.

Options. Fix each item test-first, as in LP-018. Items 11–12 change only notes.

Leaning / resolution. Daniel asked Claude to implement the fixes (2026-10-29: “You may implement it yourself”); no commits requested.

Resolution (2026-10-29, Claude)

Each item got a regression test with the input above, run and seen to fail before its fix: lib/Mycelium.jl/test/lp019.jl (items 1, 2, 5, 6, 9), lib/LenticulumCore.jl/test/lp019.jl (item 3), lib/ImplicitLayers.jl/test/lp019.jl (items 4, 10) and the top-level test/lp019_interop.jl (items 7, 8, 13; the ChainRulesCore and ExponentialFamily extensions do not load in LenticulumCore’s own test environment).

  1. RelayFactor contributes −H(q), q the pooled incoming messages, and uses_cavity is true. Relaying a categorical or Gaussian prior now leaves F=0. Behaviour change: a directed relay (Absorbing() in, Emitting() out) now raises the LP-014 error in graph free-energy evaluation; the LP-014 test now uses Bidirectional() relay edges (still F=4.5) and asserts the error for the directed graph.
  2. _mixture_product puts a proper Gaussian first whichever side it came from; both orders give [0.3543, 0.6457].
  3. Gaussian, the precision factorisation and isproper check max∣M−M⊤∣≤εFloat32​​max∣M∣ (_nearly_symmetric).
  4. _fd_step (shared by fd_jacobian and _divergence) divides by the increment the rounded probe makes; the default relative step is max(10−7,εT​​) for IEEE types, so Float64 keeps 10−7, and stays 10−7 for dual numbers. The identity’s Jacobian is exactly 1 in Float64, Float32 and Float16 (it was 1+5.8⋅10−10 even in Float64). The first version converted to Float64 and broke the stiff SciML CNF testset, which runs ForwardDiff duals through _divergence; fixed before the final runs.
  5. Damped tree schedules are iterated and checked; undamped ones keep the one-sweep shortcut.
  6. Proper overlaps use logN(μc​−μd​;0,Σc​+Σd​), improper ones an expansion about μc​ in the same convention as before. Exact to rounding at shifts 0, 106, 108; beyond ∼1012 the canonical form itself loses the mean.
  7. The pullback is (Dj​−pj​∑D)/wj​; zero at equal weights down to 1e-320.
  8. as_belief(::SampleList) unwraps zero-dimensional samples.
  9. ResidualSchedule rejects maxtasks < 1 at construction; the LP-002 adjoint test that expected the gradient driver to reject it now expects the constructor to.
  10. _norm is LinearAlgebra.norm.
  11. Messages are Inversions states cπ′​(y)=π⊙μf→v​ with the worked case, and passing.md and Backward Signatures name likelihood_message. DEQ as a Relation §5 now says the DEQ message is the fixed point, with the prior only a warm start (the old “posterior rather than a likelihood” was wrong).
  12. DEQ as a Relation §4 and The Equilibrium Family §4 say a singular I−∂z​g is the failure of the IFT’s sufficient condition, with z3=x as the counterexample.
  13. LinearConstraintFactor and NonlinearFactor use _nearly_symmetric instead of exact issymmetric.

Implementation notes updated: Mycelium factors.md, passing.md, schedules.md, gaussian.md; LenticulumCore gaussian_belief.md, interop.md, open_model.md; ImplicitLayers solve.md, flow.md.

Verification (2026-10-29). All seven suites pass with Pkg.test(): LenticulumCore, Mycelium and ImplicitLayers (both with LENTICULUM_DEVICE_TESTS=true), the top-level package, AlgebraicFactors, Adversarial and VariationalDiffusion. The vault builds (258 pages) and docs/make.jl builds. Logs: ~/.julia/lenticulum-runs/lp018-claude/lp019/.

Review of the resolution (2026-10-30, Codex)

Codex reviewed commit 29b2b18b read-only and confirmed items 1, 2, 5, 8, 9, 12 and the mathematics of every fix (the relay’s −H(q) and the LP-014 consequence; sixty dense checks of the overlap formulas; the pullback formula; the AlgebraicFactors caller of combine_with_overlap). It found nine regressions or gaps in the fixes; Claude reproduced each with Codex’s scripts (~/.julia/lenticulum-runs/review-lp019/), added a regression test, and fixed it:

  1. Item 6: the proper overlap called inv on a Cholesky factor, which Zygote cannot differentiate (dpotri foreigncall); expected derivative 0.25. Now uses the belief_cov/_precision_* helpers that have rules.
  2. Item 6: the same code scalar-indexed diagonal device Gaussians (JLArrays, allowscalar(false)). The helpers keep the diagonal device path.
  3. Item 6: an improper quadratic site far from the origin (s=108) still gave weights [1.0, 0.368], summing to 1.37: the log masses were O(s2) and the normalisation added its correction to a number of size 1016. Mixture products now re-centre each improper site at a reference point shared by all its pairs (_overlap(c, d, ref); the constant dropped depends on the site alone), and normalise by subtracting the maximum first.
  4. Items 3 and 13: the tolerance εFloat32​​ admitted [1 0.9999; 1.0002 1], whose upper triangle is definite but whose symmetric part is indefinite. The tolerance is now ε​ of the element type, and definiteness is checked on, and factorisations use, M/2+M⊤/2 (not (M+M⊤)/2, which overflows near floatmax); the constraint and nonlinear constructors store that symmetric part.
  5. Item 7: for w = [1e-200, 1e200] the new pullback lost −1/∑w=−10−200 because p1​ underflowed. Where pj​ underflows it now uses Dj​/wj​−∑D/∑w.
  6. Item 6: two Gaussian(0, 1e308) gave −∞; Σc​+Σd​ is now scaled by its largest diagonal entry.
  7. Item 4: Float16 ForwardDiff duals still got the 10−7 step (a zero increment, NaN); the step now uses eps of the dual (its primal’s), and a step that does not change x throws instead of dividing 0 by 0.
  8. Item 10: Float64(norm(v)) underflowed a BigFloat residual of 10−400 to a false root; the norm now stays in the residual’s precision.
  9. Item 11: DEQ as a Relation §5 said a point message cannot carry a likelihood; it is the likelihood of an exact constraint, δ(z−z∗). The note now says it cannot carry a finite-noise likelihood.

Second review (2026-10-30, Codex)

Codex reran its fifteen reproducers (the nine above and six on the exposure code of L-SPEC-003 Exposed Parameters and Energy Channels) against the working tree: fourteen fixed, the exposure polarity list still incomplete above its size cap. It found further defects, all reproduced and fixed by Claude with regression tests:

  • Re-centring changed relative masses between different improper sites (introduced by the fix of the first review’s item 3): the dropped constant η⊤r−r⊤Λr/2 is a constant of each site, so it cancels only when the other side has exactly one improper site. Weights for two sites against N([2,0],I) were [0.731, 0.269]; the closed form is ∝(e−1,e2). A reference is now used only in the single-site case.
  • One global scale for the proper overlap underflowed tiny variances (N(0,diag(10200,10−200)) threw); it is now a congruence scaling by max(Σc,ii​,Σd,ii​)​ per coordinate, diagonal-preserving.
  • Zygote through the diagonal device overlap scalar-indexed in the pullback of Diagonal / α; now broadcasts on diag.
  • The √eps(T) symmetry tolerance changed under ForwardDiff duals and rejected LP-002’s finite-difference adjoint perturbations (the Mycelium suite’s “LP-002 C correlated dense Gaussian message coordinates”, ~1.3e-6 asymmetry). The tolerance is back to √eps(Float32) for every type: with definiteness checked on the symmetric part, it only has to refuse gross asymmetry.
  • The symmetric part M/2+M⊤/2 rounded a subnormal diagonal to zero; it is now M+(M⊤−M)/2, exact for symmetric M and free of overflow.
  • The pullback’s shared term ∑D/(ms) overflowed for [1e-200, 1e308, 1e308]; now (∑D/m)/s.
  • The projected-moments tolerance rejected a valid Float32 rank-one belief and accepted a flat direction read with weight 10−11; it now finds flat directions by an eigendecomposition in Float64 and requires C not to see them beyond 64ε∥C∥.
  • The channel/parameter shadow rule rejected valid clamped exposure; such factors now keep the clamped path and refuse only the belief path.
  • The exposed polarity list: complete enumeration compiles one Polarity type per assignment (21.9 s for six channels). Above five channels in all, supported_polarities now throws, as L-SPEC-001 permits, and hasnopolarity/isunidirectional answer lazily.

Recorded, not fixed: SolveReport.residual is a Float64 field, so a BigFloat residual of 10−400 is reported as 0.0 (convergence is decided in full precision; pre-existing).

Third review (2026-10-30, Codex)

All fifteen original reproducers and the second round’s reproducers pass. Nine further findings, reproduced by Codex’s scripts (~/.julia/lenticulum-runs/review-followups2/) and fixed by Claude with regression tests:

  • A mixture with proper components beside one improper site still got the reference shift (weights [0.101, 0.899], closed form [0.015, 0.985]): a reference is now used only when the other side is that single site and nothing else.
  • Exposure: an information vector with zero precision (a linear site eη⊤z) was taken for no information; it is now refused where the factor reads it. The flat-direction test had an absolute floor that let a read of weight 10−16 through; it is now relative.
  • The covariance pullback returned an asymmetric precision cotangent, inconsistent with the symmetric-part forward map (Zygote −0.548/−0.025 against ForwardDiff −0.286 for both off-diagonals); it is symmetrised.
  • The weight pullback overflowed ∑D for cotangents near floatmax; D is scaled first.
  • The shadow rule refused channels named like unexposed parameters ((:x, :noise) with learned noise); the generic rule now concerns exposed blocks only, and GaussianFactor refuses only channels named :A or :b on its belief path.
  • isunidirectional above the enumeration cap traversed 36 assignments (24 s, 1.6 GB); both predicates now answer structurally, assuming a factor that accepts a block target with latent channels also accepts it with everything observed.
  • The proper overlap overflowed for means near ±floatmax; it now scales before subtracting and halves before squaring.
  • show printed an improper belief (indefinite symmetric part) as a proper Gaussian.

Still not supported, as documented: mixtures with improper components on both sides (the overlap rule needs a proper first operand), and SolveReport.residual’s Float64 field.

Fourth review (2026-10-30, Codex)

Every earlier counterexample passes. Three further findings, fixed by Claude with regression tests (scripts in ~/.julia/lenticulum-runs/review-followups3/):

  • An improper site with a skew part in Λ ([1 1e-4; -1e-4 0]) gave weights [0.378, 0.622] where its symmetric part diag(1, 0) gives [0.5, 0.5]: the expansion used the raw Λ while the factorisations used the symmetric part. It now uses the symmetric part throughout.
  • The third round’s “scale before subtracting” rounded each Float32 mean separately; means 10−6 apart with variances 10−12 lost 0.027 in the log overlap. The difference is now taken first, with the scaled form only as a fallback when μc​−μd​ overflows.
  • The structural hasnopolarity/isunidirectional of ExposedFactor disagreed with the accepted assignments for custom hooks; both are exact again, by the lazy search with early stopping (exponential when it finds none or exactly one).

Recorded, not fixed (extremes only): cotangent rescaling can give [-1, 0] where the exact pullback is zero after underflow, subnormal Float32 weights can give infinite gradients, and the symmetrised covariance adjoint can overflow where the reference is −10308.

Fifth review (2026-10-30, Codex)

Every earlier counterexample passes. Two further findings were fixed by Claude with regression tests (scripts in ~/.julia/lenticulum-runs/review-followups4/):

  • AlgebraicFactors’ branch_entropy_bounds still mirrored the upper triangle (Symmetric(c.Λ)), so after item 3 its bounds excluded the entropy Core and Mycelium report for a slightly asymmetric precision (2.98178 against 2.98168), and threw for one that Core accepts. It now uses _symmetric_part.
  • The flat-direction test in exposure’s _projected_moments used a cut-off relative to the largest eigenvalue, so a precision of 1016 on an unread coordinate made a read coordinate of precision 1 look flat. The precision is now equilibrated, DΛD with D=diag(Λii−1/2​), before the cut-off.

Accepted, not changed: exact hasnopolarity/isunidirectional on ExposedFactor visit all 3n assignments when they find none or one (Codex measured 27 s for six channels on first call); the round-four structural shortcut was wrong for custom hooks, and exactness was kept. A fast path would need factor-provided support summaries. The extreme-value limitations above are unchanged.
