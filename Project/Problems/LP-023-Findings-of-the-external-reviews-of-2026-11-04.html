open-problem

Status: partly resolved (2026-11-04). The concrete defects that the code confirms are fixed, each with a regression test. The architectural and theory proposals are recorded here and not started. Related: LP-018 Defects found in the review of the older packages, LP-019 Defects found in the review of the core packages, LP-022 Constraint factors are normalised in their residual, not their channels, passing, lens.

Sources: two source-level reviews that Daniel supplied (ChatGPT, of master at 3074717, 2026-11-03; Gemini, undated), neither of which ran code; code: lib/Mycelium.jl/src/passing.jl, messages.jl, graph.jl, cluster.jl, lib/LenticulumCore.jl/src/lens.jl, open_model.jl, src/switching.jl, lib/VariationalDiffusion.jl/src/implicit.jl, answers.jl, lib/ImplicitLayers.jl/src/deq.jl, neuralode.jl; tests: listed per item

Problem. Two external reviews listed defects, risks and proposals. Neither reviewer executed Julia, and both say so. Each finding was checked against the code on dev-danielb (newer than the reviewed commit) before anything was changed.

Checked and not changed

  • Missing parameter keys resolve to empty trees (ChatGPT P2). True (_subtree), and data factors rely on it, so the lookup is unchanged. Since 2026-11-04 validate_parameters(g, ps) checks a tree against the nodes’ initial parameters (missing keys, names, array sizes, frozen paths; unread keys warn); test/validate_parameters.jl. It is not called by inference itself.
  • Root deduplication with a fixed tolerance (Gemini 2.1). implicit_roots uses a relative unique_tol (default 10−3), a keyword. Scaling it by the local conditioning, as proposed, is plausible but not measured; no failing case is known.
  • Bethe, EP and surrogate free energies should be named apart (ChatGPT 4.3). The EP free energy has its own stationarity test (test/mixture_rules.jl). A uniform report of which objective a number is remains open.
  • Fallback to fixed-message gradients (ChatGPT 4.2). That is the documented fallback for factors without factor_belief (LP-020), and it warns. Exposing the gradient semantics in the result is a proposal.
  • Implicit-relation calibration (ChatGPT 4.4, Gemini §5). Stable zeros of a smoothed score are modes of pt​, not points of the data manifold; lib/VariationalDiffusion.jl/examples/circle_mlp.jl was the one manifold example. Since 2026-11-05 lib/VariationalDiffusion.jl/examples/calibration.jl measures it on the circle, the lemniscate and unbalanced rings (calibration.md). Every query outside a relation’s range returns a root, the lemniscate’s recall is at best 0.52, and a 10% ring is never found. The proposed metrics (distance to the manifold, spurious roots, branch recall on a lemniscate, a pitchfork, a redundant arm, unbalanced mixtures) and remedies (annealing in t, ridge residuals, normal-bundle parameterisation) are research items, not defects.

Proposals, not started

  • Typed, compiled execution plans grouped by factor type (both reviews); runtime symbol lookup in graph-wide parameter trees (ChatGPT P1). Parallelism and Compilation already records the latter.
  • A dependency-aware priority queue for ResidualSchedule (each sweep now probes every task, O(T2) evaluations).
  • Trait types instead of Bool traits, a promotion lattice for combine, Val channel names (Gemini §1): plausible; no measured cost.
  • Pre-allocated message buffers (Gemini §3); batched multi-start roots; matrix-free Newton–Krylov as the default above a dimension threshold.
  • A lazy product-of-potentials belief; incremental (iSAM2-like) inference; heteroscedastic and heavy-tailed likelihoods; a second Julia version and device tests in CI (ChatGPT §5–6).
