LP 009 Game level free energy is declared but not implemented

LP-009 Game-level free energy is declared but not implemented

27 Oct 202610 min read

  • open-problem

open-problem

Status: resolved within the agreed bounds + exact-cases scope (2026-10-27); general games outside that scope remain explicitly unsupported. The 2026-10-23 resolution listed methods that did not exist. Related: L-SPEC-001 The Factor Interface §4–§5, statistical_game, abstract_types, factors.

Problem. LenticulumCore declares the game-level entropy(f, π, y, ps, st) and free_energy(f, π, y, ps, st) (AutoBayes Definition 20), and abstract_types.jl lists entropy among the methods a factor must provide. At the time this problem was opened, no factor implemented either. Every factor that is used in a graph implements Mycelium’s local_free_energy(f, msgs, ps, st) instead, which takes all incoming messages because a graph has no distinguished prior/observation split. Then scalar_free_energy(f, π, y, ...) failed for every factor in the repository, the same kind of gap as issue #8.

Options.

  1. Derive the game-level free energy from local_free_energy, by passing the prior as the message on the unobserved channel and a Dirac belief on the observed one.
  2. Implement free_energy and entropy per factor.
  3. Drop them from the required list, and document local_free_energy as the free energy of a factor.

Resolution so far. Option 1 for the five Lenticulum factors (GaussianFactor, GaussianPrior, LinearConstraintFactor, NonlinearFactor, LikelihoodFactor): free_energy passes the prior and the observation to Mycelium.local_free_energy, and since 4a49ca5c entropy returns an element of the energy space, not a bare number. PriorFactor has exact game-level methods for Dirac and categorical beliefs (bb3af9e9); for a Gaussian belief with an arbitrary nlogp they throw, because the expectation has no closed form. The 2026-10-23 resolution also listed a generic LenticulumCore fallback and methods for EqualityFactor, DeterministicFactor, the equilibrium and neural-ODE factors; none of those methods existed at review (LP-013 Defects found in the review of master). Correction (2026-10-26): EqualityFactor and DeterministicFactor are not types in this monorepo (rg over the Julia sources finds no occurrence); they are not outstanding implementations. DEQFactor and NeuralODEFactor do exist; the incoming/solved distinction is now implemented at factor and assembled-lens levels, as recorded below.

Algebraic family (L-SPEC-004; history through 2026-10-25, superseded below). ToricFactor has exact game-level quantities when normalised; PluckerFactor and PolynomialFactor do not. Polynomial single-branch messages now supply an assembled local lens and Gaussian/GN sites, but freezing a different metric at each target/iteration does not by itself define a normalised joint branch model or exact entropy. No game or graph-local free energy is claimed for it. The full common conformance checker therefore remains inapplicable; all supported degree-one polarities have independent lens/site/posterior checks in lib/AlgebraicFactors.jl/test/polynomial.jl (polynomial).

Decisions and checks, 2026-10-26

  • DataFactor and RelayFactor: zero free energy and entropy, matching their declared clamped-source/identity accounting. Their pointwise methods now expose the same zero.
  • PriorFactor: existing exact Dirac/categorical games retained; pointwise nlogp(x) exposed.
  • LossFactor: terminal game, all channels supplied as named point/Dirac observations y; energy is the loss and entropy is zero with the loss’s scalar/vector shape. No inversion or polarity is claimed.
  • OptimiserFactor: stateful reparametrisation/update rule; statistical game quantities are undefined and now throw clear ArgumentErrors.
  • LuxFactor: conditional point inversion, constant-zero energy and zero point-belief entropy.
  • GeneratorFactor: same zero pair for a Dirac latent observation y; NoiseSource: same pair conditioned on a fixed Dirac draw π with nsamples=1, without resampling. Particle entropy is not supplied; unsupported game domains throw ArgumentError.
  • RatioFactor: with a Dirac prior, reweighting preserves the point, so energy is -logratio and entropy zero. Particle game priors still throw because no entropy reference measure has been chosen (LP-006). LP-016 now supplies an extrinsic likelihood potential and averages the graph energy contribution under the reweighted factor belief.
  • PluckerFactor: trivially extendable forward point game, zero energy/entropy for named Dirac input observations. Its existing direct message path passes the common checker without a lens. Nonpoint game observations throw. No inverse fibre law is supplied.

Every implemented domain above is tested against local_free_energy with option-1 messages (the unary prior/fixed draw, or prior plus Dirac observation; terminal Loss has all observations and a trivial prior). Shared checker fixtures use closed forms; package tests also assert the actual energy/entropy values, including nonzero Prior, Loss and Ratio energies.

Item 1 resolved: incoming factor and solved-point lens games (2026-10-26)

The maintainer chose two levels. DEQFactor and NeuralODEFactor now implement option 1: π is a Dirac on the first channel, y a Dirac on the second, matching GaussianFactor’s fixed argument order. This is the first-unobserved/second-observed split regardless of the polarity used for inversion. Factor free energy delegates to local_free_energy; entropy is zero in ScalarEnergySpace(). Other beliefs throw with a reason.

For lens = first(assemble(f, p, ps, st)), free_energy(lens, π, inputs, ps, st) instead scores the point returned by that polarity’s inversion: residual energy plus the prior’s anchor cost. This is Definition 20’s expectation under the inversion, with zero point-belief entropy. A trivial prior has no cost. A hard Dirac prior at a different point costs infinity. A proper Gaussian anchor costs ½(u*-μ)'Λ(u*-μ), relative to its mean (no normalization constant). A finite channel precision does not soften a Dirac prior. Soft observations, singular Gaussian anchors and other priors throw. The existing solve/flow inversion is unchanged; scoring a prior does not turn it into a prior-conditioned posterior.

For a residual affine in the unknown u, vanishing at u*, the incoming energy equals the solved residual energy plus the anchor centered at u₀, with precision J_u'J_u. energy_jacobian computes that precision in lib/ImplicitLayers.jl/test/conformance.jl. For g=z/2+x, x=1,z₀=3 gives root 2, Jacobian 1/2, and ½(3-2)²/4=1/8; reverse z=3,x₀=1 gives root 3/2, Jacobian -1, and ½(1-3/2)²=1/8. For dz/dt=0, endpoints 1,3 give ½(3-1)²=2 in either direction. The nonlinear test derives a cubic energy gap under the first-order Gauss–Newton approximation; see deq and point_game. These replace the old disagreement assertions and remove both factors’ game_gap exemptions. Arbitrary-point scoring remains energy/scalar_energy (L-ADR-0003 Pointwise energy and free energy are separate levels), not a keyword on free_energy.

Remaining, with reasons (original item numbers retained):

  1. DiffusionFactor: graph (clamp,score) uses a denoising surrogate, not posterior entropy; its missing-channel heuristic also reconstructs a polarity with a trivial prior. An exact Definition 20 entropy/free energy cannot be recovered by wrapping that code. Fixed-node quadrature removes randomness but does not supply the missing semantics.
  2. Ratio’s non-Dirac priors and Generator/NoiseSource/Plucker distributional games need a density/entropy law; no new approximation is licensed here.
  3. PolynomialFactor has no normalized joint branch model or entropy, and no graph-local free energy. Existing independent lens/site/posterior tests remain unchanged.
  4. ComposedFactor and TensorFactor have no channels, inversion or evaluated energy/game laws. Core tests verify that the common checker cannot treat them as wired factors. Composition requires pushforward/downstream expectation; tensor requires joint input and belief conventions. Value-level composition helpers do not implement these operations.

The problem remains open for these precise domains. No methods are claimed for fictitious types, and no arbitrary expectation or posterior-entropy approximation has been added.

Items 2–5: bounds plus exact cases (2026-10-27)

The maintainer’s decision of 2026-10-26 is implemented within the following explicit scope. This section supersedes the earlier remaining-item descriptions without claiming the general distributional games are solved.

  1. Diffusion: free_energy_upper_bound evaluates the negative ELBO of an explicitly normalised finite VP reverse chain on full joint point evidence. Terminal N(0,I), all reverse Gaussian variances, decoder and normalisers are specified by the API; the variational law is the forward chain conditioned on evidence. A single analytic Gaussian predictor has an exact expectation and returns FreeEnergyUpperBound(value). General predictors use estimate_free_energy_upper_bound, returning a different FreeEnergyBoundEstimate(value,standard_error,nsamples); a sampled estimate need not itself lie above negative log evidence. Neither type is a number/ordinary energy. lib/VariationalDiffusion.jl/test/bound.jl compares with exact Gaussian likelihoods: the stationary analytic score saturates the bound, and the gap decreases as a displaced score approaches it. The graph (clamp,score) surrogate is not a normalised ELBO; exact free_energy/entropy and partial-evidence bounds still throw. See bound.
  2. Generators and source: AffineGenerator with a proper Gaussian latent law and a square nonsingular map implements exact Lebesgue entropy H(g∗​p)=H(p)+log∣detA∣. The unconditional forward game has zero map energy and free energy −H(g∗​p); output conditioning is rejected. InvertibleGenerator carries a caller-certified global bijection and exact log-Jacobian callback; nonlinear Gaussian-source expectations are separately labelled EntropyEstimates, never exact game entropies. Dirac/finite supported laws are Lebesgue-singular even if a Jacobian expectation can be a finite exact sum. Non-invertible maps throw. The built-in standard-normal NoiseSource has exact unconditional entropy without resampling; custom samplers still supply no density law. Tests: lib/Adversarial.jl/test/transport.jl, including Gaussian closed forms, a nonlinear bijection with analytic Jacobian expectation, and common conformance for the affine Gaussian game. See transport and generator. Ratio: non-Dirac games remain unsupported: arbitrary ratio networks have no analytic normaliser/reweighted entropy contract, and empirical weights are not a Lebesgue density. Plucker: an exact distributional case does exist at r=2, with one fixed nonzero vector and one proper Gaussian: its scalar image is Gaussian. Entropy/free energy and direct-message conformance are checked in lib/AlgebraicFactors.jl/test/game_exact.jl. Higher-dimensional fixed-vector images are singular; two Gaussian inputs are generally non-Gaussian. Jitter is an approximation and is refused by the exact game. See plucker.
  3. Polynomial: degree one with branches=:single, a square nonsingular last-channel block, SPD constant residual covariance and a proper pooled posterior defines an exact normalised linear-Gaussian conditional. Its normaliser includes the output log-Jacobian. Game/graph quantities use the Gaussian grading; π is on the first channel and y names all others. lib/AlgebraicFactors.jl/test/game_exact.jl derives the closed form, checks both polarities with the common checker, and checks basis/gauge/damping cases. Other degrees, branch mixtures and unsolvable normalised output blocks throw. See polynomial; no local metric is promoted to a nonlinear density.
  4. Composition and tensor: a pair of GaussianFactors integrates out its shared channel, giving A=BA1​, b=Ba1​+b2​, Q=BQ1​B⊤+Q2​. Endpoint messages, games, entropy and graph contributions match that collapsed Gaussian factor. Both endpoint polarities pass common conformance and independent posterior/entropy tests. Independent TensorFactor games accept products explicitly as NamedTuples keyed by parts; each entry describes the corresponding complete channel game. Branch-graded results scalarise to the sum. Correlated joint beliefs and undeclared/nonlinear components throw. Tensor graph wiring, nested composed chains and composition with noise hyperpriors remain unsupported. Tests: test/game_composition.jl. See composition and statistical_game.

The interface amendment is appended to L-SPEC-001 The Factor Interface, not made into an ADR. The original conditional point games and their conformance coverage remain in place. The unsupported domains above are deliberate boundaries of this resolution, not hidden approximations.
